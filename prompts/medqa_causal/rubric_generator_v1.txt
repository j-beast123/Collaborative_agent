You write one behavior rubric for a collaborating clinical Agent, from one localized interaction failure. The rubric must
correct the Agent's DECISION BEHAVIOR at the transition turn; it must not teach any medical knowledge.

You receive the dialogue up to and including the transition turn, with every answer option replaced by a label
(HYPOTHESIS_P = the Agent's prior leading hypothesis, HYPOTHESIS_C = the collaborator's hypothesis, HYPOTHESIS_O1/O2 = others),
the Agent's state per turn, the primary causal behavior, and an abstract description of the transition.

Hard rules:
- No disease name, no option label, no finding, no disease-specific fact, no hint about which hypothesis is right. Refer to
  "the Agent's current leading hypothesis", "the collaborator's competing hypothesis", "the most discriminating evidence".
- The behavior must be reusable in any case with a competing collaborator hypothesis.
- Score 2 must require an observable decision action, not only a procedure: the Agent COMPARES the two hypotheses on the
  most discriminating available evidence, CHOOSES one of maintain / revise / unresolved by an explicit rule, and STATES that
  decision explicitly (an explicit primary conclusion, or an explicit "unresolved" with the specific discriminator requested).
  A score-2 behavior that ends in checking, considering, clarifying or distinguishing is not acceptable.
- Score 1: the comparison is made but no explicit decision follows (or the decision does not follow the rule). Score 0: the
  behavior that failed (e.g. adopting the collaborator's hypothesis without a comparison, or hedging between both).
- Address the primary causal behavior you are given.

Fields (all required, plain sentences):
- name
- user_side_context: the collaborator-side situation in which the rubric applies (the trigger, in general terms)
- agent_side_state: the Agent-side state before its reply (its current leading hypothesis and how it judges the evidence)
- trigger, comparison_action, decision_rule, explicit_commitment, revision_condition, uncertainty_action
- score_0, score_1, score_2: the behaviors, each starting "In this situation, the agent ..."
- ideal_response_behavior: one instruction to the Agent for its reply in this situation (behavior only)

Return exactly one JSON object with these keys and no Markdown.
