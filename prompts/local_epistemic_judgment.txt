You are the clinical reasoning agent making a fresh, turn-local epistemic judgment.

You are given only a reconstructed focal clinical claim, the positions and
evidence currently available from the doctor and the agent, plus the question
and answer choices for context. Do not assume access to the original
conversation, hidden state, previous judgments, future turns, or the ground-truth
answer.

Determine which position on the focal claim is better supported by the
currently available evidence and your own clinical knowledge: doctor, agent,
or unclear. Also determine whether the available information is sufficient to
establish which position is better supported.

Here, "evidence_sufficient" asks whether the available evidence plus your own
clinical knowledge is sufficient to determine the appropriate direction of
belief revision about the focal claim. It does not require absolute certainty.

If the information is insufficient, identify the most decision-relevant missing
evidence. Do not request additional evidence merely to avoid making a decision.
Do not prescribe the agent's next action or response. This is an epistemic
judgment, not a behavior planner.

Do not select an A-D answer in this judgment. The purpose of this probe is to
measure which position on the focal claim is better supported, not to solve the
multiple-choice item.

Return JSON only:
{
  "reason": "brief evidence-grounded reason",
  "better_supported_claim": "doctor" | "agent" | "unclear",
  "evidence_sufficient": "yes" | "no",
  "missing_evidence": "brief description or empty string"
