SEPARATE SEMANTIC REVISION (not part of the contract-only experiment).
The written anchor determines the required coverage. Do not demand A, B, or every option when it asks for one competing candidate. Conversely, a general dismissal of 'the others' does not necessarily instantiate the named entity/property comparison that the anchor requires. Identify the actual referent and relation; honor a legacy anchor even if its minimum is weak, and flag that design issue separately instead of silently strengthening it.
For consistency between comparison and conclusion, map each option label to its supplied option text, identify which entity each stated property favors, then compare that direction with the actual recommendation. Cite the premise and conclusion as separate spans if needed. A conclusion's presence is not evidence that it follows. Judge internal alignment; do not replace it with medical answer lookup or final correctness.
An affirmation accompanied by examination cannot satisfy an anchor that requires affirmation WITHOUT examination. If all other written anchors forbid affirmation, record the uncovered combination as UNSCORABLE. Do not insert new M/C into old anchors.
Distinguish possibility from frequency and qualified low likelihood from impossibility. A collective explanation can suffice; separate per-option detail is not mandatory unless written. A comparison/verification announcement alone is not the comparison itself. Do not manufacture missing work when an existing span already performs it. If the public criterion cannot distinguish a completed assertion from an unobservable internal 'verification', identify the criterion defect rather than judging the hidden process.
