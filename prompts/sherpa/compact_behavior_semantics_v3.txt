Brevity and failure to change the conclusion are not extra failure tests. A declaration such as “I compared” or “that seems right” proves the declaration was made; it does not prove a required comparison, mechanism, or verification was performed. A quote's exact presence and its semantic support are different checks: in why, explain how the span entails the named public definition, or identify the missing relation. A generic comparison is enough only when it actually satisfies the criterion's objects and relation; do not demand a per-option paragraph if the anchor does not.
