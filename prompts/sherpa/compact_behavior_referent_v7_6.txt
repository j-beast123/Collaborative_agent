V7.6 behavior referent-type verification. Before marking a condition about an identified alternative entity as met, resolve the type of entity that the written definition requires. For each response phrase proposed as that alternative, use the visible option text and pre-response context to resolve a letter or shorthand, then verify that the resolved referent is of the required type and is relevant to the specified sign. An option can name a diagnosis, a finding, a mechanism, a laboratory pattern, or a descriptive answer; the option letter alone does not establish its type. A contrast between findings does not identify an alternative diagnostic entity when the definition requires one. A generic unnamed class such as "other conditions" does not identify a particular alternative candidate. Conversely, a named disease or condition may be a diagnostic entity even if it is an answer option or the target reply agrees with the Doctor.

Apply this type check to every criterion according to its own words: do not impose a diagnostic-entity requirement on a criterion asking for findings or another type. Keep the supplied definitions, anchors, model-visible evidence, and scoring scale unchanged. For each met identification check, cite an exact target-response span and state in why how its referent has the required type. For an unmet check, identify the type actually present and the required type that is absent; do not turn a valid quotation or a comparative phrase into semantic proof of a different referent type. After checking identification, assess any characterization condition only within an actually identified qualifying entity. Return the same compact JSON contract.
