The system writes one rubric for each trajectory or reuses a same-reason rubric within that case and outcome stratum. Actual witness and KEEP protocol:

KEEP occurs at the first passing sampled support; otherwise the rubric is NOT_VALIDATED after the sampled set (at most 10 trajectories) is exhausted. NOT_VALIDATED means no witness was found under this bounded protocol, not that the behavior is universally ineffective.
- The prompt-search effect score reuses these sampled witness results; it does not rerun every member. Tested source-success and source-failure trajectories receive their observed target-hit fraction; known invalid/no-match cases contribute zero; unexamined members are omitted.
