- stated in terms of the physician's expressed reasoning, uncertainty, evidence, request, disagreement, commitment or the

require knowing that a claim, association, inference or choice is correct or incorrect.

- 1: the behavior is partially present or incomplete.
- 2: the behavior is done in the way that best supports a correct decision.
