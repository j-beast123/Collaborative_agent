Verify only the proposed contradiction_flag and unverified_fact_assertion tags
for each dialogue. All outcomes are hidden. Judge observable assistant behavior,
not success, medical correctness, or whether a physician changed their answer.
Read the full dialogue and the supplied case. Return each candidate tag exactly
once: either as an accepted behavior with its supporting assistant evidence_id,
or in rejected_tags. Do not introduce tags that were not candidates.

contradiction_flag requires an EXPLICIT assistant statement identifying a
conflict between two assertions or between a premise and a conclusion. A
different preference, generic qualification, or the assistant making internally
contradictory statements is insufficient. In particular, "this is a risk factor,
so it is the exception" can itself be contradictory without POINTING OUT a
contradiction. Accept only when the assistant identifies what fails to follow
or what conflicts, even if the assistant later handles that insight poorly.
The evidence passage must belong to the turn that actually flags the conflict.

unverified_fact_assertion is narrowly about unestablished CASE-SPECIFIC facts:
patient findings, medication history, exposure history, visual features, acuity,
gestational timing, or similar particulars asserted as observed or known. Also
accept when the assistant treats missing case information as evidence of absence.
Reject ordinary general medical knowledge claims, including a claim about where
a drug comes from, usual anatomy, a guideline, drug safety, typical treatment,
or a disease mechanism. Such a claim may be wrong but it is NOT an invented
patient/history/image fact, and this analysis does not perform medical fact checking.
If the question supplies no patient or image context and the assertion is only
general medical knowledge, reject this tag. Conditional hypothetical statements
and information already supplied in the case or prior conversation do not qualify.
Do not treat timing of a symptom as proof of timing or speed of an intervention.

Use only the ASSISTANT's passage IDs to support accepted tags. Do not award
credit for physician speech, and do not borrow evidence between dialogues.
Return JSON only: annotations with dialogue_id, behaviors (tag, evidence_id),
and rejected_tags. If unsupported, reject rather than guessing.
