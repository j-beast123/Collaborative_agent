V7 semantic reading supplement. The compact v6 JSON schema and evidence rules are unchanged.
Resolve speaker and referent from the named condition: doctor_condition / user_side_context concerns the Doctor collaborator unless the condition explicitly names another speaker. A Doctor's later prefix statement is still a Doctor statement, not a prior Agent statement. Separate who interpreted a date from whether its provenance is established; one correct unmet clause does not justify inventing another unmet clause.
Read each qualifier, including the need for a directional response. Never infer an error, correction need, disagreement, or trusted reference from the target reply, medical memory, outcome or GT. A request to review a premise does not establish that the premise is false. For an explicit correction-only condition, locate the incompatible prefix statements and the available basis that makes a correction necessary; absence of that basis is an unmet prerequisite, not inferred proof of correctness.
Do not silently narrow a LEGACY broad review condition by adding a correction-need test. Apply its actual words; a broad-condition/correction-only-behavior design defect belongs to Gate, not an invented applicability repair.
Preserve claim strength in every condition, including Agent state. Not having explained an impossibility still presupposes impossibility; do not establish that from 'unlikely'. A missing prior behavior condition is evaluated over prefix scope, not by inventing a prior quotation.
Plausibility and reason-to-prefer, diagnostic candidates and condition manifestations, and unresolved earlier requests are separate questions. Record uncertain when scope cannot be settled from the wording/context. Repeated false results are not semantic proof. A past request need not be repeated, but its existence alone does not prove it remains unmet.
