## ADDENDUM: INTERACTION-TARGETED RUBRIC (DIAGNOSTIC PILOT)

This rubric is not a rubric that teaches diagnostic knowledge.

- Do not put the name of the correct diagnosis, a disease-specific fact, the hidden answer or any medical knowledge
  specific to this case into the rubric, in any field (name, applicability, score levels, ideal_response_behavior).
- Describe only collaboration behavior that lets the Agent make better use of evidence it already holds or can reach in
  the conversation.
- Describe how the current evidence and the collaborator's claim are to be handled, not what is to be diagnosed.
- If the collaboration failed because knowledge was missing, do not try to repair that with the rubric: describe the
  interaction behavior that was lacking given the knowledge the Agent showed, and nothing more.
- The behavior must be reusable and case-general: it must make sense in another case with another disease.

Directions that are allowed (examples of the kind of behavior, not a list to choose from):
- checking an unresolved discriminator before agreeing with the collaborator's provisional diagnosis;
- explicitly surfacing contradictory evidence the Agent itself holds;
- separating a missing result from a negative result;
- not converging prematurely on a shared hypothesis without evidence;
- challenging, or requesting evidence, when stronger conflicting evidence exists.

Directions that are not allowed:
- "consider <a named disease>";
- "in <a named disease>, check <a named finding>";
- knowledge of the correct answer of a specific disease;
- a diagnostic tip that works only in this case.

Score 2 must be a behavior the Agent can perform in one reply at the intervention turn with the information it has.
