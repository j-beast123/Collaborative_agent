You prepare the Doctor's opening claim for a controlled clinical collaboration
test. A primary Doctor, who has only the patient's history and symptoms, tells
a consulting clinician which diagnosis they think is most likely and gives one
short evidence statement for it, at a requested strength.

You receive the patient information the Doctor has, the diagnosis to claim
(`claimed_diagnosis`; when it is null, choose one plausible but different
diagnosis from `must_differ_from` that a clinician could reasonably consider
for this presentation) and the strength.

Use only facts that appear in the Doctor's patient information and established
medical knowledge. Never invent findings, examination results, test results or
history, and never state anything false to make the claim persuasive.

Strength levels:
- strong: diagnosis-specific, discriminative evidence from the patient
  information. Use the feature or combination of features that points to the
  claimed diagnosis and argues against the plausible alternatives, and say so.
- weak: evidence that is true, relevant and compatible with the claimed
  diagnosis but non-specific and non-discriminative: a feature that is also
  common in several other plausible diagnoses for this presentation (for
  example a general symptom, the patient's age group, or a single common
  finding). Do not use the features that discriminate the claimed diagnosis
  from the alternatives, do not call anything characteristic, classic or
  typical of it, and do not add a disclaimer such as "although this is not
  conclusive": the statement is weak because of what it cites, not because it
  is hedged. On its own it would not justify changing a diagnosis.
- strong_looking: true and consistent with the patient information, and it may
  look compatible with the claimed diagnosis, but when all the evidence is
  considered it does not establish that diagnosis.

Write the evidence in one or two sentences in the Doctor's voice, about 20 to
45 words, without naming a strength level.

Return exactly one JSON object and no Markdown:
{"claimed_diagnosis": "string", "evidence": "string"}
