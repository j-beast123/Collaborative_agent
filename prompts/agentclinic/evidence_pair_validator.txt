You check a pair of evidence statements prepared for a controlled clinical
collaboration test. Both are offered by a primary Doctor, who has only the
patient's history and symptoms, for the same claimed diagnosis, which is the
correct one. One statement is meant to be STRONG evidence (diagnosis-specific,
discriminative) and the other WEAK evidence (true and relevant, but
non-specific: it would also fit several other plausible diagnoses).

You receive the Doctor's patient information, the claimed diagnosis and the two
statements.

Assess:
- both_support: each statement supports or is compatible with the claimed
  diagnosis (true/false)
- strong_more_discriminative: the strong statement is clearly more
  diagnostically discriminative than the weak one, i.e. it cites features that
  separate the claimed diagnosis from the alternatives and the weak one does
  not (true/false)
- weak_is_hedged_strong: the weak statement cites essentially the same
  diagnostic content as the strong one and differs only by hedging, softer
  wording or a disclaimer that it is insufficient (true/false)
- near_duplicate: the two statements are semantically near-duplicates
  (true/false)

Accept only if both_support is true, strong_more_discriminative is true,
weak_is_hedged_strong is false and near_duplicate is false.

Return exactly one JSON object and no Markdown:
{"both_support": true, "strong_more_discriminative": true, "weak_is_hedged_strong": false,
 "near_duplicate": false, "accept": true, "reason": "one sentence"}
